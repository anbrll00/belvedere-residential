Cleaning, Explained

Deep clean or recurring clean?

The terms get used loosely, so here's exactly what's included in each — and why every first visit starts with a deep clean.

Best for biweekly or monthly upkeep · 2–2.5 hrs on site

Recurring Clean

  • Kitchen counters, stovetop, sink & outside of appliances
  • Bathrooms — toilet, sink, shower/tub, mirror
  • Floors vacuumed & mopped throughout
  • Visible surfaces dusted
  • High-touch spots — doorknobs, light switches
  • Trash emptied, visible clutter straightened
Best for a first visit or seasonal reset · 4–5 hrs on site

Deep Clean

Everything in a recurring clean, plus:

  • Baseboards & door frames, wiped not just eyeballed
  • Inside the oven, fridge & microwave
  • Window tracks & sills
  • Behind & under movable furniture
  • Light fixtures & ceiling fan blades
  • Grout scrubbed in bathrooms
  • Vents & air returns dusted

Why your first visit is always a deep clean

On a brand-new home, we don't know the real baseline yet. Starting with a deep clean resets the house properly, so every recurring visit after that stays fast, predictable, and fairly priced.

Moving in or out? That's a deep clean plus empty-house extras — every cabinet, every closet, no furniture in the way — which is why it's priced a step above a standard deep clean.
